# DA PDF Design & Production Standard

Character-led educational PDFs. Proposed production specification, Version 1.0, October 2026.
Extends the DA Brand & Style Authority and Visual System V2; it does not replace them.

Learn the rules. Question who wrote them.

## Principle

> Mabbit and Whonah don't appear in DA resources because they're the brand. They are the brand because of what they do. They ask better questions, demonstrate useful methods, draw attention to what matters, and help learners become more independent.

DA PDFs are underground academic field manuals that Mabbit and Whonah assembled to teach something useful. They are not worksheets with a logo, not children's activity books, and not generic study guides. Everything is freely accessible.

## 1. Look

- **Cover:** Void Black, Anton uppercase, one leading fluorescent accent, distressed paper or print texture, approved character art.
- **Layered academic aesthetic:** photocopy artifacts, filing tabs, stamped labels, circled evidence, taped excerpts, handwritten margin notes.
- **Teaching pages:** readable body text; explanations, activities, examples and references clearly separated.
- **Character-led sequences:** Mabbit and Whonah use poses that match the instructional action.
- **Surprises:** a sarcastic note, evidence stamp, character reaction or unusual composition, never at the cost of accessibility.
- **Physical-document feel:** pages look assembled, marked up and investigated, not generated from a slide template.

Foundation layouts from the brand guide: **The Verdict**, **The Evidence Board**, **The Annotated Artifact**.

## 2. Colors (locked)

| Color | Hex | Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Void Black | `#0D0B12` | Covers, contrast, redactions, headlines |
| Paper White | `#F5F2EA` | Main reading surfaces and worksheets |
| Electric Violet | `#5B16D9` | Critical analysis, conceptual depth |
| Acid Lime | `#B7FF18` | Actions, checkpoints, findings |
| Hot Pink | `#FF168C` | Disruption, critique, Mabbit's emphasis |
| Signal Cyan | `#10D9D0` | Evidence, definitions, organization |

One fluorescent accent dominates a composition, with at most one smaller supporting accent. Use the approved lighter/darker contrast registers where accessibility requires. No new palette.

## 3. Mabbit and Whonah must teach

- **Mabbit** is the investigator and critical instructor: frames the problem, teaches concepts, questions conventional explanations, challenges weak evidence, works examples, demonstrates revision. Skeptical and sometimes cutting, never at students for not knowing something.
- **Whonah** is the active-learning partner: draws attention to details, demonstrates sequences visually, helps identify evidence, marks checkpoints, introduces hands-on exercises. He acts and reacts; he does not become a talking cartoon dog.
- Whonah stays a realistic German shepherd with approved markings and proportions. Mabbit keeps her established details (purple hair, tattoos, earrings, outfit, adult proportions). Never recreate either from a generic prompt. Use the approved Finals.
- Their actions drive the activity; they are not just talking about it.

## 4. Recurring teaching components

| Component | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Mabbit's Second Look | Critical questioning: what is presented as normal, who established it, what supports it? |
| Whonah's Field Notes | Practical instruction: a short actionable sequence or annotated demonstration. |
| The Evidence Board | Investigation: a source, claim, comparison or example opened up and examined. |
| Try It Yourself | Guided practice after the characters demonstrate. |
| Check Your Work | Formative feedback: criteria, model responses, explanations. |
| The Final Verdict | Synthesis: the finding, the limits of what can be concluded, the next action. |

Recognizable across resources, not forced into every document. A two-page reference sheet does not need what a twelve-page workbook does.

## 5. Anatomy of a guided PDF (flexible, 8 to 12 pages)

1. Case File Cover: bold claim, title, characters where appropriate, topic, clear free-resource label.
2. The Briefing: what the learner will accomplish, why, what they need.
3. Mabbit Investigates: the central problem, a challenging question, the concepts.
4. Whonah's Field Notes: a usable strategy in manageable actions.
5. 5-6. The Evidence Board: a substantial worked example with character-led annotation.
6. 7-8. Your Turn: guided practice with room to write.
7. Check Your Work: criteria, common pitfalls, corrective guidance.
8. The Final Verdict: recap, reflection, next action, sources.

Further pages may hold references, extra examples, accessible worksheets or optional practice. Sequence follows learning needs, not a mandatory count.

## 6. Visual treatments by resource type

- **The Case File** (guided academic toolkits): dark, high-impact covers; clipped evidence, redaction bars, restrained distressing, an Acid Lime verified stamp. Lighter reading surfaces inside.
- **Cut & Paste** (playful activities, printables): physical collage, marginal notes, character reactions. Adapt thoughtfully; do not turn educational pages into social graphics.
- **Field Guide** (reference sheets, institutional navigation): precise, numbered, cyan-structured, clear steps, credible references. For students, tutors, librarians, advisors, instructors.

## 7. Pilot: Decode Your Assignment

Learning outcome: the reader can identify an assignment's required task, evidence expectations, constraints, evaluation criteria and next steps. The PDF must demonstrate what counts as important, explain why, and give a way to check the result, not just say "highlight important information."

## 8. Technical standards

- Real selectable text and accessible structure. Never flattened page images.
- Pipeline: instructional brief; evidence and content development; character-led teaching script; layout and asset placement; accessible export (searchable text, semantic headings, reading order, image alt text, accessible tables, metadata); QA and publication.
- Keep source content, character art, layouts and exports separate so a resource can be revised without rebuilding.
- US Letter (8.5 x 11 in), generous margins, consistent page numbers, clear activity space. Minimize ink on interior pages.
- Selectable text, working links, document metadata. Interactive fields optional; must remain usable printed.
- Target PDF/UA where tools support it, and test with assistive technology rather than assuming a tagged export is accessible.

## 9. Review gates

| Review | Questions |
| --- | --- |
| Academic quality | Claims accurate, sourced and nuanced? Does the example demonstrate the method? |
| Learning design | Can someone reach the goal with this resource alone? Practice and feedback present? |
| DA brand fidelity | Approved assets? Sounds like DA? Character involvement purposeful? Colors, type, layouts correct? |

Fail a resource if removing Mabbit and Whonah changes nothing about the teaching. Do not confuse integral teaching with character art on every page; give reading space, evidence, citations and student work room.

## 10. Never

- Generic corporate worksheets with mascot decoration.
- AI-generated replacements of Mabbit or Whonah.
- Pages of character dialogue without instruction.
- Infantilizing encouragement or shallow explanations.
- Fictional citations, fake institutional documents presented as real, invented evidence.
- Dense decorative backgrounds behind teaching text, or branding that overwhelms the worksheet.
- Ink-heavy elements that serve no purpose.
- Donation demands, paid upgrades, gated answer keys.
- Unverified claims about what all professors, universities or disciplines require.

## 11. Free-resource model

Each resource identifies itself as a free DA educational resource. After the instructional content and sources, include a small voluntary-support note:

> KNOWLEDGE ISN'T A LUXURY PRODUCT. This resource is free because useful educational tools shouldn't depend on someone's ability to pay. If it helped, you can support Deconstructing Academia through an optional contribution or by checking out Mabbit & Whonah merchandise when it returns. NO PURCHASE REQUIRED. KEEP LEARNING.

Do not insert invented payment links. During the Etsy pause, do not advertise products as currently available.

## 12. Final production checklist

- Specific, achievable educational purpose; begins with a compelling question or real problem.
- Established DA visual identity; approved, lesson-appropriate Mabbit and Whonah art.
- Characters actively teach; a method is demonstrated and practiced; useful checks or feedback.
- Appropriate references and honest evidentiary boundaries.
- Readable on a phone and practical to print.
- Selectable text, accessible reading order.
- Fully usable without paying, subscribing, donating or purchasing.
- Reviewed for accuracy, character fidelity, layout quality and technical function.
