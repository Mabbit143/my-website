This article exists to show what the article template looks like with real content in it — headings, a pull quote, a list, and a link — not to make any actual claim.

## What a real article needs

When you're ready to replace this with a launch piece, each article should include:

- A headline and deck that state the real claim in the first two lines.
- The series it belongs to (this one is filed under **Whose Peers?**, just as a demo).
- A body that names the actor when the evidence supports naming the actor.
- Sources or further reading, when the piece draws on outside material.

> Pull quotes render like this — in the punk/annotation voice, set apart from the body copy.
